### 1. What should Bridge provide?
 * **Selected Option:** **Use existing stablecoins (recommended)**

   * You avoid the compliance burden, technical overhead, and smart contract risks associated with issuing your own proprietary custom tokens.
### 2. How should KES and Privy balances interact?
 * **Selected Option:** **Keep separate; transfer only after explicit approval (recommended)**
 * **What it means:**
   * **KES (Kenyan Shillings)** represents local fiat currency balances stored via local payment gateways (like PesaPal), while **Privy** manages user-embedded web3 wallets holding crypto/stablecoins.
   * **Keeping them separate** creates a strict guardrail between local fiat money and on-chain assets.
   * Funds will **never** automatically swap or bridge between KES and stablecoins without the user explicitly initiating and confirming a transaction (e.g., clicking "Convert KES to USDC"). This prevents unexpected gas fees, slippage, or accidental cross-asset movements.
### 3. What should Stellar SDP pay?
 * **Selected Option:** **Admin-approved, prefunded batches (recommended)**
 * **What it means:**
   * The **Stellar Disbursement Platform (SDP)** is built for bulk payouts or disbursements (e.g., paying out yields or earnings to investors).
   * **"Admin-approved, prefunded batches"** means all outgoing payouts are batched together and require explicit human admin review before any money moves. Additionally, the disbursement account must already hold sufficient pre-deposited funds.
   * This prevents automated "runaway" payouts, safeguards against malicious API triggers or client-side tampering, and ensures that funds are available before execution.
